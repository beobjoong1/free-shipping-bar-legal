Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026. This policy explains what the Wix app "Free Shipping Bar Goals" ("the app"), provided by operix apps, does with data. It applies to site owners who install the app and to shoppers who see the bar on those sites.

What the app does

The app shows shoppers on a Wix Stores site how much more they need to add to their cart to get free shipping (and, on the paid plan, other goals the site owner sets). It appears in the side cart, and optionally on product pages and in a site widget.

Data the app stores

Settings. The free-shipping amount, goals, wording, colors and link chosen by the site owner, and the app plan last confirmed for the site, are stored in a collection inside that site's own Wix CMS.

The app stores nothing about shoppers.

Data the app reads but does not store

The shopper's cart. To show the remaining amount, the app reads the shopper's current cart through Wix: the type of each item (for example physical, digital, gift card), whether it ships, its price and the currency. This is used only to calculate the bar at that moment and is not saved.

The site's shipping rules and plugin placement. In the dashboard, the app reads the site's Wix shipping rules to show whether the bar's amount matches the free-shipping amount Wix applies, and reads where its site plugins are placed. These are not saved.

The app does not place cookies, does not track shoppers across pages or sites, and does not read customer lists, addresses, payment details or members' private data.

Where the data lives and for how long

Settings are stored in the site's own Wix CMS, under the site owner's control, and processed on Wix's hosting infrastructure. The site owner can change them at any time in the app's dashboard. Wix removes the app's collection when the app is uninstalled.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is processed by Wix (as the platform hosting the site, the cart, the CMS and the app backend) under Wix's own terms and privacy policy.

Your rights and contact

Shoppers should contact the site owner about their orders. Site owners can contact us at beobjoong@gmail.com to ask about the app's processing or raise a complaint. Where GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA apply, you have the rights those laws provide, including access, correction and deletion.
