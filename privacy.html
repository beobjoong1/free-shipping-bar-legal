Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026. This policy explains what the Wix app "Free Shipping Bar Goals" ("the app"), provided by operix apps, does with data. It applies to site owners who install the app and to shoppers who see the bar on those sites.

What the app does

The app shows shoppers on a Wix Stores site how much more they need to add to their cart to get free shipping (and, on the paid plan, other goals the site owner sets). It appears in the side cart, and optionally on product pages and in a site widget.

Data the app stores

Settings. The free-shipping amount, goals, wording, colors and link chosen by the site owner, and the app plan last confirmed for the site, are stored in a collection inside that site's own Wix CMS. This includes, on the paid plan, any free-shipping amounts the site owner sets by country.

The app stores nothing about shoppers on its side. On sites that set free-shipping amounts by country, a shopper who chooses their country on the bar has that choice (a two-letter country code) kept in their own browser's local storage, so the bar remembers it on that site. It is sent only with the bar's own request.

Data the app reads but does not store

The shopper's cart. To show the remaining amount, the app reads the shopper's current cart through Wix: the type of each item (for example physical, digital, gift card), whether it ships, its price and the currency. This is used only to calculate the bar at that moment and is not saved.

Where the shopper is (paid plan, amounts by country). To pick the right amount, the app uses, in this order: a shipping address the shopper typed on their cart (only its country, and whether a street or city is filled in), the country the shopper chose on the bar, the country Wix itself puts on the cart from the shopper's location, and the time zone name of the shopper's browser (for example "Europe/Berlin"), which the bar sends with its request. These are used only to choose the amount for that request and are not saved. Sites without amounts by country do not use them.

The site's shipping rules and plugin placement. In the dashboard, the app reads the site's Wix shipping rules and shipping regions (which countries each covers) to show whether the bar's amount matches the free-shipping amount Wix applies, and reads where its site plugins are placed. These are not saved.

The app does not place cookies, does not track shoppers across pages or sites, and does not read customer lists, payment details or members' private data. Of a shipping address it uses only the country, as described above.

Where the data lives and for how long

Settings are stored in the site's own Wix CMS, under the site owner's control, and processed on Wix's hosting infrastructure. The site owner can change them at any time in the app's dashboard. Wix removes the app's collection when the app is uninstalled.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is processed by Wix (as the platform hosting the site, the cart, the CMS and the app backend) under Wix's own terms and privacy policy.

Your rights and contact

Shoppers should contact the site owner about their orders. Site owners can contact us at beobjoong@gmail.com to ask about the app's processing or raise a complaint. Where GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA apply, you have the rights those laws provide, including access, correction and deletion.
